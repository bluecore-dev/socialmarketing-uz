📜 SCROLL ANIMATSIYALAR (INTERSECTION OBSERVER)

Har bir section elementlari scroll'da chiqayotganda:

FADE + SLIDE UP:
  opacity: 0 → 1, translateY(60px) → translateY(0)
  duration: 0.9s, easing: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

BLUR TO CLEAR:
  filter: blur(12px) → blur(0), opacity 0 → 1
  duration: 0.8s

SCALE IN:
  scale(0.92) + opacity 0 → scale(1) + opacity 1

STAGGER CARDS:
  Har biri 0.12s delay bilan ketma-ket chiqsin

PARALLAX (YENGIL):
  Section backgroundlari scroll tezligida 
  0.3–0.5x nisbatda sekinroq harakat qilsin
  (pure CSS transform yoki requestAnimationFrame)
